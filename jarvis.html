Jarvis

This page identifies the Jarvis app (client ID https://www.ai-council.co.uk/jarvis) when it asks to connect to your Home Assistant instance using Home Assistant's OAuth-based login flow.

Authorization disclosure

  • Jarvis signs in through your own Home Assistant login page. Jarvis does not collect, see, or store your Home Assistant username or password.
  • After you approve access, Home Assistant returns you to the app at jarvis://auth.
  • OAuth tokens stay local, on the device running Jarvis. They are not sent to ai-council.co.uk.
  • This page has no scripts, analytics, API calls, or credential inputs.

You can revoke Jarvis's access at any time from your Home Assistant user profile.
